01The hook: «tú dices → te sale»

Flowlet is not another transcription app, and the video should not say it is. Three demo formats that only Flowlet can do, in this order of strength:

  • Spoken correction, live. You say «quedamos a las 3, perdón, a las 4» and the text comes out «Hemos quedado a las 4.» The mic test nobody else passes.
  • Filler removal. Ramble with «eh», «este», «en fin ya sabes» — the strikethrough version on top, the clean sentence under it. Same visual language as the Play frames.
  • Any app. WhatsApp, Notes, Docs: the bubble writes where the cursor is, no clipboard, no app switching.

Give the creator the before/after pair verbatim (the few-shots on useflowlet.com are already copy-approved in ES and EN) so the demo is honest and the output on screen is real.

02The blocker: today, viewers cannot redeem anything

go / no-go

Flowlet is in Play closed testing (internal + alpha), not in production. A personal developer account needs 12 closed testers opted in continuously for 14 days before "Apply for production", then ~7 days of review. Internal testers do not count.

Code redemption requires a Play install. /promo/redeem demands a Google account and a Play Integrity verdict of PLAY_RECOGNIZED, which only Google gives for installs from Play. A sideloaded APK cannot redeem, and nobody outside the closed test can install at all.

So: either time the collabs for production day (recommended: line the creators up now, publish when the gate clears), or make the video a waitlist pitch ("comment and I'll get you in") and hand codes out later. Do not promise instant access in a video — it would backfire.

04How to find the rest

  • Search strings that returned real handles: site:tiktok.com/@ + niche words («AI tools», «apps», «oposiciones», «tdah»); Toklytics and Socialpruf profile pages give handle + size; Feedspot creator lists; «5 cuentas de TikTok y Instagram para organizar tus estudios» (Emagister) and thekingofcontent.agency for Spanish rates.
  • Hashtags on TikTok Creative Center: #aitools #appfinds #techfinds #productivityhacks #truquillos #appsqueutil #androidtips #oposiciones #estudio #tdah
  • Sourcing habit: the fastest pipeline is the comment section of Wispr Flow, Otter and Gboard voice-typing videos — people asking "what app is that" are creators and power users.
  • Vetting: 3–8% engagement on TikTok, 3–6% on IG; comments that look like conversation; no follower spikes; audience in a Play-supported locale you can serve (es/en); and at least one video showing an app on screen. Skip iOS-only creators — Flowlet is Android.

05What to offer

  • Creator: their own code FLOW-HANDLE with a stack of credits (two 7h packs is generous and costs you pennies at LLM cost), plus the referral upside.
  • Audience: code gives +30 min on redemption. Existing referral already pays 1 h to both sides — reuse that machinery; do not invent a new reward path.
  • Money: for nano/micro, gifted credits + their own code is often enough; expect €50–150 if they want cash for a dedicated video. Seed many small ones (20–50/month) rather than buying one big name.

06Rules so this does not backfire

  • Never trade credits for Play reviews (house rule, and Google's policy). The code buys usage, not ratings.
  • Declare it. Gifted/paid must be disclosed (#publi, «regalo») — EU rules and TikTok/Meta branded-content tools both require it. Undisclosed is how accounts get restricted.
  • Pay by effect, not by posting. Paying for stories/shares is "incentivized engagement" — against Meta's rules and a risk for @useflowlet. Redemption of a unique code measures the same thing without breaking platform rules. Meta's own brand-partnership rules are the line here.
  • One code per creator, always — promo_redemptions then tells you exactly which creator moved installs, and you double down on that niche.
